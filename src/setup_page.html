Connect to Twilio

The phone uses your Twilio account to make calls. You need an account with one phone number (Twilio charges about $1 a month for the number plus a few cents per minute).

  1. Open console.twilio.com in another tab and log in.
  2. On the home page, find Account Info.
  3. Copy the Account SID and the Auth Token (click Show first) into the boxes below.

Used once to set things up, then forgotten. The phone never stores it.

International format: start with + and the country code, for example +1 555 123 4567 or +84 90 123 4567.

Setting up

    Try it

    Press Test call (or the phone's button) and pick up the handset. The phone calls your contact.

    Hold the phone's button for 10 seconds to erase all settings.
