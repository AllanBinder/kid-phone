Who the phone calls

Each button on the phone calls one person, and the light next to it is on when they can be called. You can fill in one to four.

Call length

When a call reaches this length the phone hangs up and says "Time's up". The child can press the button again to call back.

Connect to Twilio

The phone uses your Twilio account to make calls. You need an account with one phone number (Twilio charges about $1 a month for the number plus a few cents per minute).

  1. Open console.twilio.com in another tab and log in.
  2. On the home page, find Account Info.
  3. Copy the Account SID and the Auth Token (click Show first) into the boxes below.

Used once, then forgotten: the phone never stores it. Instead the phone keeps a limited Twilio key that can only update the list of numbers it may call, so changing names, times or phone numbers later does not need the token.

Setting up

    Try it

    Press a Call button (or the button on the phone) and pick up the handset.

    WiFi networks

    The phone remembers up to 5 WiFi networks and joins the strongest one it can see. Taking the phone to another home? Add that home's WiFi here first, while the phone is still on this one.

    Recent connection attempts

      Since the phone last started. Signal is what the phone saw just before the attempt.

      Add a network

      Pick one from the scan, or type the name (for a hidden network). The phone uses 2.4 GHz WiFi only.

      Advanced

      Factory reset

      Erases everything on the phone: the WiFi, the Twilio connection, the people on each button, their times and the call length. Nothing in your Twilio account is deleted.

      Afterwards the phone is off every saved WiFi network, so this page stops working. Set it up again from the start: plug its COM port into a computer and open the installer page.

      You can also erase all settings by holding the BOOT button on the phone's board for 10 seconds.
